Rubik’s Cube Solver

Pick a size, paint every face to match your cube, then solve.

Cube size
Paint colour (click or drag over stickers)

Hold the cube with any face up and any face towards you, then paint the net as if it were unfolded: up above front, left and right beside front, back beside right, down below front. On a 3×3 any colour scheme works — paint the centres too. On a 2×2 use white up, green front.

Solution

Clockwise face turns; ’ is counter-clockwise, 2 is a half-turn. Drag the cube to rotate your view, tap any step or use the slider to jump there, and use the left and right arrow keys to step back and forward.
